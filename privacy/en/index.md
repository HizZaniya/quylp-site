# Quylp Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

This policy describes how Quylp, a desktop application for Windows ("the app"), handles your information.

## Information We Collect

The app does not collect any personal information. It does not send your information to the developer or to any third party.

The app has no account registration, usage analytics, advertising, automatic crash reporting, or automatic update checks.

## Network Communication

The app does not connect to the internet while you work. The images you import and the ebooks you export are processed entirely on your PC.

The only time the app opens a page on the internet is when you click the Ko-fi link in the lower-left corner of the window. The app then opens the Ko-fi page (<https://ko-fi.com/hizzaniya>) in your default browser. That page is an external site and is governed by Ko-fi's privacy policy. The app sends no information to Ko-fi.

## Data Stored on Your PC

The app stores the following on your PC. None of it is sent anywhere.

- Settings: language, theme, output settings, the output file name template, and saved presets
- Recently opened files: the locations of files and folders you opened, up to the 10 most recent
- Window state: size, position, and whether the window is maximized
- Log: the app version, the OS type, and details of errors that occurred while the app was running. Error details may include file locations
- Images extracted from PDFs: when you open a PDF, these are placed temporarily in a working folder and deleted when the app exits

These are kept in the app's own folder (your per-user application data folder in Windows). Deleting that folder deletes what the app has stored.

When you choose to save, the app saves the edit file and the exported ebook to the location you choose. When you export the log from "Info and log" in Preferences, the app copies it to the location you choose. The app never sends these files anywhere.

## Microsoft Components and the Store

The app uses Microsoft Edge WebView2 to display its window. If you got the app from the Microsoft Store, the Microsoft Store handles installation and updates. Information handled by these is governed by the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

## Children's Information

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

## Changes to This Policy

If the app's behavior changes so that this policy no longer matches it, this page will be updated and the effective date revised.
